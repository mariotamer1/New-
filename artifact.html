About Rescue Drywall

Drywall is the surface everything else depends on.

Rescue Drywall repairs, restores, replaces and finishes drywall. The goal on every job is the same: the repair should be impossible to find once the light hits the wall.

Damaged drywall is rarely just a surface problem. A crack usually follows a seam or a stud line. A soft ceiling means water found a path. An impact hole leaves crushed gypsum well past the edge of the dent. So we cut back to sound material rather than filling over failure, fit new board to the framing, and rebuild the surface the way it was originally built — tape, then compound in coats, each one wider than the last.

Finishing is where a repair is won or lost. Compound is feathered out far enough that the transition disappears, sanded flat to the surrounding plane, and the existing texture is matched before anything is primed. Corners get bead and a true line. Seams get flat tape and a full three-coat build, not a single skim.

Cut back to sound material

Crushed and water-softened gypsum is removed, not buried. Repairs are squared to framing so the patch has something solid to hold.

Coats, not shortcuts

Tape, fill, then feather. Compound is built up and sanded between coats so the seam ends up flat instead of crowned.

Texture that matches

Knockdown, orange peel, smooth level finish — the surrounding wall sets the standard the patch has to meet.

    Rescue Drywall handles interior drywall work on walls and ceilings: drywall repair, drywall restoration, drywall replacement, hole and crack repair, water-damaged drywall repair, seam and joint repair, and drywall finishing.
