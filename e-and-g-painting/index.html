About E.G. Painting

With 5 years of industry experience, E.G. Painting has proudly served homeowners and commercial clients since 2021. We specialize in professional interior and exterior painting, cabinet painting and refinishing, drywall repair, custom trim and millwork, decks, siding, and other residential and commercial improvements. From detailed preparation and repairs to complete property transformations, we bring quality craftsmanship, dependable service, and attention to detail to every project. Our goal is simple: deliver exceptional results that our customers can be proud of.
