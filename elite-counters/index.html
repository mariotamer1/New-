What we do

Four trades, one crew,
one person accountable.

Most kitchens get handed between four contractors who never meet. Juan runs stone, tile, cabinetry and paint himself — so the counter, the backsplash, the doors and the trim are measured against each other, not against a stranger's guesswork.

Template

We measure the real space — walls are never square. Digital template or story stick, seams planned where they disappear.

Fabricate

Slab laid out for vein match, cut, edge profiled, polished, sink cutout and faucet holes bored to the fixture.

Set

Cabinets shimmed level, counter set and seamed, backsplash laid to a dry-run layout so cuts land where the eye doesn't.

Finish

Caulk, grout, hardware, touch-up paint. We leave the site clean and walk the whole job with you before we go.

About

Juan has been doing
this for over ten years.

Elite Counters is built around one person's hands and one person's name. Over ten years in stone work, tile, cabinets and painting — long enough to have seen how a job fails, and to have learned the slower way that keeps it from failing.

That means the person who quotes your kitchen is the person who templates it, sets the counter, and comes back if something isn't right. No rotating subs, no handoffs between trades, no one pointing at the last guy.

Every estimate is free, and it comes with a straight answer about what the space actually needs — including when the honest answer is less work, not more.
