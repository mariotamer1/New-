How it goes

Four steps, one person
answering for all of them.

Most kitchens get handed between trades that never meet. Here the counter, the backsplash, the doors and the trim are measured against each other.

Template

We measure the real space — walls are never square. Seams planned where they disappear.

Fabricate

Slab laid out for vein match, cut, edge profiled, polished, cutouts bored to the fixture.

Set

Cabinets shimmed level, counter set and seamed, backsplash laid to a dry-run layout.

Finish

Caulk, grout, hardware, touch-up paint. Site left clean, and we walk the job with you.

About

Elite Counters is built around one person's hands and one person's name. Over ten years in stone work, tile, cabinets and painting — long enough to have seen how a job fails, and to have learned the slower way that keeps it from failing.

The person who quotes your kitchen is the person who templates it, sets the counter, and comes back if something isn't right. No rotating subs, no handoffs between trades.

Every estimate is free, and it comes with a straight answer about what the space needs — including when the honest answer is less work, not more.
